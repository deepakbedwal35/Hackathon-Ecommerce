# Store Return and Refund Policy

## General return policy
Customers may request a return and refund for eligible items within 30 days of the recorded delivery date. Requests initiated after the 30-day return window will be automatically rejected. Items must be in their original packaging, unused, and accompanied by the relevant order number or identifier.

## Refund amounts and partial refunds
Refunds can be requested for the full order amount or for partial amounts corresponding to individual items or partial claims. The requested refund amount must be positive, specified as an exact decimal with at most two decimal places, and cannot exceed the remaining refundable balance of the order. Any refund request exceeding the remaining refundable balance will be rejected.

## Non-returnable items
Certain categories of items are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable due to health, hygiene, and perishable goods regulations. Orders containing items in the grocery category are strictly non-returnable. If an order contains any non-returnable items, refund requests for that order will be refused under standard policy.

## Electronics
Electronics, including audio equipment, peripherals, gaming accessories, and smart devices, are eligible for return within the standard 30-day window from the delivery date. Items must include all original cords, adapters, documentation, and accessories.

## Fashion
Apparel, footwear, vintage clothing, and fashion accessories are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached. Returns for incorrect sizing or fit are fully supported within the eligible return period.

## Home
Home goods, drinkware, kitchenware, and living accessories can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Items must be undamaged and in original condition.

## Beauty
Cosmetics, personal care, and beauty products are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery only if unopened, unused, and with seals intact.

## Books
Physical books, educational materials, and literature are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery, provided they remain in clean, unread, or undamaged condition.

## Grocery
Perishable food items, beverages, organic teas, and grocery products are strictly non-returnable and non-refundable once delivered. Under store safety regulations, grocery items cannot be accepted for return or refund.

## Refund timeline
Once a refund is approved and processed, funds are transferred back to the customer's original payment method. The refund appears in 5-7 business days depending on banking and card issuer settlement cycles.

## High-value refunds
Refund requests for amounts strictly above INR 5,000.00 cannot be automatically approved by the automated system. Any refund or cumulative refund exceeding INR 5,000 requires human review by our specialist support staff. When a high-value refund is submitted, an escalation ticket is created and reviewed by staff before any funds can be disbursed.

## Already refunded orders
An order or portion of an order that has already received a processed refund cannot be refunded again. If an order has been fully refunded, subsequent refund requests will be rejected immediately as already refunded.
